FAQ

Questions.

Do you need access to my Seller Central or Shopify admin?

One permissions-scoped seat on whichever platform you run — that's the whole setup. On Amazon: Reports (view) lets the models read your numbers; Pricing and Campaign Manager (view & edit) let us make the moves. On Shopify: a collaborator account with Orders, Analytics, Reports and Marketing to view, and Products and Discounts to edit — nothing under Settings, Finances or Payouts. Banking and account settings stay untouchable on both, and you revoke the seat in one click, any day. Prefer not to grant it? Send a handful of CSV exports through a secure upload page instead.

What data do I need to send?

About fifteen minutes of exports, once. On Amazon: the Business Report, SKU Economics, your advertising reports and an FBA inventory snapshot. On Shopify: orders, products with cost per item, payouts, and your Meta or Google ads exports. Your private upload page lists each one with the exact clicks to get it. The one thing no platform knows is your costs: a one-row-per-SKU sheet (unit cost, freight, fulfilment, lead times), five minutes, estimates welcome. After that the seat does the rest.

What exactly does the Profit Record count?

Every move we make — a price step, a search term cut, a reorder, a claim filed — written down with the dollars we expect before it goes live, then measured from your own later exports: on the exact line, capped at what we promised, misses included, Amazon claims only when Amazon pays. Attribution tells you which ad made the sale; the Profit Record tells you which decision made the profit. It is also what voids our invoice, and you can export it any day. An Amazon claim sits under "found" from the day we file it and moves to "proven" only when Amazon pays — never before. Reorders are recorded, never claimed as dollars — a stockout you didn't have can't be measured. A fourth outcome exists: "unmeasurable" — we could not isolate what a move earned, so nothing is banked and the row says so. Month one, your elasticity says "not enough data." Month six, every price, reorder and ad budget in your account has a receipt behind it and a confidence interval under it.

Can anything move in my account without my yes?

On the 45-minute kickoff call you get your 90-day plan: quarterly targets for profit, revenue, and margin, plus the three or four moves that get there. On the same call you set the bounds we act inside without asking — and exactly where your veto sits. After that: one email, three days before anything moves, with every planned move and its expected dollars. Say no to any of them by reply. Silence after three days is a yes only for moves inside the bounds you set on the kickoff call — a bigger price step, a new campaign or a reorder waits for your written yes — and if three weeks pass without one it lapses rather than going live. If the email announcing a planned move doesn't go out, nothing moves — the veto window only opens on a sent notice, and that is code, not policy.

No obligation, and nothing renews against your will.

Why $3M?

The floor comes from the guarantee. An invoice stands only if your Profit Record covers everything billed since day one. On the inputs published in the pricing breakdown — 20% of ad spend leaking, 1% of FBA revenue recoverable, ad spend scaled from the $25k-a-month account on $5M — a $2M brand has roughly $3,600 a month to find, and a $3M brand roughly $5,400.
