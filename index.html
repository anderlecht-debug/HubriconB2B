Method, assumptions, and what public data can't see
  • Read from the listing's public page on September 3, 2026: price band, item weight, dimensions (large standard size) and sales rank (top 100 in Automotive). Priced on September 30, 2026.
  • Fees: Amazon's published 2026 US fulfilment schedules, January 15 to October 14 and October 15 to January 14, plus the 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge; a 12% referral fee.
  • The item weight is a floor. Amazon bills the packed weight, so the unit sits at least this far past the edge.
  • Volume: about 3,600 units a month from the public sales rank, a curve that can be wrong by half or double; simulated that wide. This one listing: an estimated $0.5M to $1.6M a year in sales.
  • Landed cost: assumed at 25% of price, drifting 10% either way each month. No public page shows it.
  • Simulation: 10,000 years of twelve months from October 2026, each month on the card in force. Year-end profit: P10 $230,000, median $402,000, P90 $687,000.
  • Storage and aged-inventory rates: Amazon's schedule effective January 15, 2026.
  • What public data can't see: advertising, returns, the real landed cost, the packed weight, and how long each unit has sat.

Questions worth asking.

How do you know which dollars you made?

Every move is written down with its expected dollars before it goes live. Afterwards each dollar is graded on your own reports: direct (Amazon paid it), isolated (on the exact line the move named), attributable (against a stated counterfactual), or unmeasurable, which banks nothing. A dollar counts once, and never above what we promised.

What counts as profit?

Money that reaches your payout because of a move: a fee you stopped paying, ad spend that stopped burning, a price that keeps more per unit, a claim Amazon paid. Misses stay on the Record at what they actually earned.

Why isn't this too good to be true?

Because the bill waits for the proof. Your first month is free. After that, no invoice goes out until your Record shows more than we've billed since day one. We can afford that only because we take on accounts where the math clears.

What happens in a month that underperforms?

Nothing you have to ask for. If the Record hasn't covered the bill, the invoice is void before it reaches you, and the work carries on. Leave any day with one email; anything billed beyond what your Record shows comes back to your bank.

What happens to my data?

We ask only for the reports the work needs, through one scoped seat or an upload. Never sold, never shared with another client. Export it any day; ask and it's deleted within thirty days.
