How a card gets here. A correction is stated with its expected dollars before it goes live. Later, the client's next exports are read back and each dollar is graded — confirmed by the platform's own record, measured on the exact line, or measured against a stated counterfactual — and capped at what was promised. Reimbursements count only when Amazon paid a claim we filed. The client then decides, on a private page, whether the result may be published. A withdrawn consent removes the card instantly.
