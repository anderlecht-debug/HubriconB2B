How every number here is priced

The fee stack
Referral = price × Amazon's published rate for the category (min $0.30). Fulfilment = the row of Amazon's US FBA schedule for your size tier, shipping weight and price column (<$10 · $10–50 · >$50), plus the 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge in force since 17 April 2026. Size tier from the packed dimensions and the greater of item weight and dimensional weight (L×W×H ÷ 139, past one cubic foot).
The cliff
The heaviest band edge below your weight, on your tier's ladder, and the fee difference between the two rows. Named only when you are within 2 oz of it; a redesign is not a finding. The price-band version is two published rates and the price you set: (price − edge)(1 − referral) − fee step.
Break-even ACoS
(price(1 − referral) − fulfilment − landed cost) ÷ price. Ads break even on profit, not on revenue. The price floor is the price at which that contribution is zero: (cost + fulfilment) ÷ (1 − referral).
15 October
The same row on Amazon's 2026 holiday peak schedule (15 Oct 2026 – 14 Jan 2027). The price that holds your margin flat is price + Δfee ÷ (1 − referral).
Monthly volume
A power-law fit of units to top-level sales rank, scaled per category. It is wrong by a factor of two either way, so every monthly figure is a bracket of half to one and a half times the estimate, and the simulation draws volume from a lognormal with that spread.
Ten thousand months
10,000 draws of monthly units (lognormal, σ = ln 2 / 1.645) and landed cost (±10%), times the unit contribution on the card. What is shown is the 10th, 50th and 90th percentile of monthly profit and the share of months that lose money.
Your category
Every listing our crawl has read and weighed in the same Best Sellers category, measured the same way, in half-ounce buckets above the band edge. Shown only from 30 listings up; nothing about any listing but yours leaves the server.
Shopify: one order
Shopify Payments takes its plan rate plus 30¢ on the whole charge, shipping included; a store not on Shopify Payments pays the plan's gateway surcharge on top. Shipping cost is the USPS Ground Advantage commercial row the parcel bills at — flat under a pound, then the whole pound above it — shown for every zone because a merchant's zone mix is not public. What is kept is a range between the nearest and farthest zone.
Shopify: the pound
USPS rounds anything over a pound up to the next whole pound. A parcel at 16.5 oz bills at two pounds; the same parcel at 15.9 oz bills at the flat sub-pound rate. The saving is the difference between those two rows, by zone; named only within 3 oz of the boundary.
Shopify: the anchor
Compare-at minus price is subtraction, not estimation. Whether it is a promotion or a policy depends on how much of the catalogue is marked down (read from the store's public catalogue when it answers) and on a fortnight of readings, which this page does not have — so it is shown as a gap, and the full Teardown decides.
Break-even ROAS
goods revenue ÷ (charged − payments − shipping − landed cost × units), per order, as a range across zones. The price floor is the unit price at which that contribution is zero.
