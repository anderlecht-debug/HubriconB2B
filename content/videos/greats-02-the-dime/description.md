Woolworth's Kept Its Dime Ceiling for more than 50 years. Here's What It Never Told Them

$127.65 in nickels, every sale the same price. That's what Frank Woolworth's store took on its first day. His company kept a ceiling over its prices for more than 50 years. It built the tallest building in the world. It also hid a number.

A price you've never moved isn't a safe price. It's an unmeasured one, and the most famous price in American retail stayed unmeasured for decades. You can do this week's steps by hand for a few products, and it's worth doing.
