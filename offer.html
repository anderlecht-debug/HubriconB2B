The offer

More profit than our bill every month, or you don't pay.

Your agency is paid on spend.

Your software is paid to report.

We're paid on proof.

Why we can say it. Every month is measured on its own exports before any invoice exists. A month that clears the fee is billed. One that doesn't is free: no credit, nothing carried. So we only take on accounts where the arithmetic clears the bill, and if yours doesn't, we tell you on the call.

$6,000 a month, flat.

Never a percentage of ad spend. Month to month. Your first month is free.

  • Every SKU modeled, every week: true margin, demand, stockout odds.
  • Every leak found and sealed: prices, ad bleed on Amazon, Google and Meta, reorders, fee cliffs, and on Amazon, claims.
  • Every dollar graded on your own reports, misses included.
  • Inventory timed against stockouts, and on Amazon, against the fee cliffs.
  • Amazon, Shopify, or both: one engine reads either, from your own exports.

The guarantee, in four layers

1

The Proving Month is free.

Your first month costs nothing, whatever it measures. There's no card on file to charge.

2

Proven or void, every month.

Each month is measured on its own exports. Above the fee, it's invoiced. At or below it, it's free: nothing credited, nothing carried.

3

Service until proven.

A month that doesn't clear isn't invoiced, and the work carries on.

4

The open exit.

Leave with one email. Any month you paid for that doesn't clear on its own number is refunded in full, issued within seven days of your email.
