Sales & traffic by product

One file per month, last 6 months — monthly files are what let us model your trend, not just a snapshot.

Seller Central → Reports → Business Reports → Detail Page Sales and Traffic by Child Item → set the range to one month → Download CSV. Repeat per month.

SKU economics (fees)

Same months as above — this is where your true Amazon fees live.

Seller Central → Reports → SKU Economics → one month per file → Download CSV.

FBA inventory snapshot

Orders

Every order and line item for the last 6 months — this is the sales, refund and discount history the models run on. We read ten columns (dates, status, refund total, SKU, quantity, price, discount) and store a per-SKU monthly aggregate; no customer name, email or address is ever written to our database. Delete those columns first if you'd rather they never leave your store.

Shopify admin → Orders → Export → Selected date range (set it to the last 6 months) → Plain CSV file → Export orders.

→

Products & inventory

Today's export does two jobs: it's your stock snapshot, and if Cost per item is filled in it's also your unit costs.

Shopify admin → Products → Export → All products → Plain CSV file → Export products.

Payouts

The processing fee on every charge and refund. Without it we estimate your fees from the published rate; with it we use what you were actually charged. Last 90 days is ideal.

Shopify admin → Finances → Payouts → Transactions → date range → Export → Transactions CSV.

Your costs

No platform knows what you actually pay for your product — margin math is impossible without this. Download the template, fill in one row per SKU (estimates are fine), upload it back. If your Products export already carries Cost per item, that covers unit cost; this template adds freight, packaging, pick/pack/postage and lead times.

Inventory age
