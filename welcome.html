Answers you shouldn't have to ask for

When do I actually pay?

Never in the Proving Month. After that, only for a month that cleared the fee. When a month ends, your Profit Record measures what every move earned in it, on that month's own exports. More than $6,000, and that month's invoice arrives by email: ACH, net-7, $6,000 flat, no card on file, nothing renews on its own. $6,000 or less, and the month is free: no invoice, no credit, nothing carried. Cancel with one email, effective immediately. When you leave, we check every month you paid for once more, and any that does not clear comes back to your bank.

What can you change in my account, exactly?

Only what your standing yes allows (the terms call it the standing mandate). That means bounded price steps, capped at 5% per SKU per two-week cycle with your Buy Box (Amazon) or conversion rate (Shopify) watched while the step is live, plus ad corrections. Anything bigger needs your explicit yes first. And you can say no to any move before it goes live.

What does the free month cost me, really?

Once your Profit Record shows proven value, we ask you for two things, once, on a private page: a short testimonial, and permission to publish your results with the numbers anonymized. Each is a separate yes, and no is fine. That's the whole price. On that page is also a link of your own: a founder you send gets the same free month, and if they stay past their own thirtieth day, one month of your fee is credited against your next invoice (or a free month is added if you are not yet invoiced), applied when their first invoice is raised after that day and never before (terms §9).

How do I revoke access?

Amazon: Settings → User Permissions → remove the user. Shopify: Settings → Users → Collaborators → remove. One click, effective instantly, and the service pauses until a seat is back. Your data and your full Profit Record export are yours, free, any time, including the day you cancel.

Who do I talk to?

The founder. Always. Reply to any Hubricon email and it lands with the person who builds your models and answers for every brief. There is no support tier.
