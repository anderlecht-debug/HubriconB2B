Answers you shouldn't have to ask for

When do I actually pay?

Never in month one. Stay past day 30, and your first invoice arrives by email. ACH, net-7, $6,000 flat. There's no card on file and nothing renews on its own. Cancel with one email, effective immediately.

What can you change in my account, exactly?

Only what your standing mandate allows. That means bounded price steps, capped at 5% per cycle with your Buy Box watched daily, plus ad corrections. Anything bigger needs your explicit yes first. And you hold a veto on everything.

What does the free month cost me, really?

If we deliver, we'll ask you for two things. A short testimonial, and permission to publish your results with the numbers anonymized. That's it. That's the whole price.

How do I revoke access?

Settings → User Permissions → remove the user. One click, effective instantly. And your data plus your full ledger export free, any time. Yes, including the day you cancel.

Who do I talk to?

The founder. Always. Reply to any Hubricon email and it lands with the person who builds your models and records your briefs. There is no support tier.
