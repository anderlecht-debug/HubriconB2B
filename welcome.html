Step 1 · The only thing we ever need from you

Add one seat. That's it.

This one seat lets our models read your numbers. It's also how the fixes happen inside your account. No report exports. No monthly homework.

Amazon: Seller Central → Settings → User Permissions → Invite new user

When the invite goes through, grant exactly these permissions and nothing else:

  • Reports → Business Reports: view
  • Reports → Fulfillment reports: view
  • Pricing: view & edit
  • Advertising → Campaign Manager: view & edit
  • Everything else stays off. Banking, settings, account info. Locked out by design.

On Shopify instead? Reply to your welcome email with your store URL (and your collaborator request code, if your store sets one under Settings → Users → Security). We send a collaborator request from the same email above; you approve it under Settings → Users → Collaborators and grant exactly these:

  • Orders, Analytics, Reports, Marketing: view
  • Products and Discounts: edit (that is how a price step happens)
  • Settings, Finances and Payouts stay off. A collaborator account never touches them.

Selling on both? Grant both seats; the models read each channel on its own and the briefs say which one a number came from.

Want it gone? One click on that same screen revokes the seat, any day you choose. And the narrow scope is the whole point. You hand over the work without handing over the keys.

One tiny homework, one time. Neither Amazon nor Shopify knows what you actually pay for your product (Shopify's cost per item is a start, not the landed cost). So your welcome email includes a simple cost template (unit cost, freight, lead time) and a secure upload link. One row per SKU, five minutes tops. Estimates are totally fine.

Rather send files than grant a seat? No problem. Reply to your welcome email and we'll set you up with a secure upload link. It works just as well. It's just fifteen minutes of exporting we'd rather do for you.

Answers you shouldn't have to ask for

When do I actually pay?

Never in month one. Stay past day 30, and your first invoice arrives by email. ACH, net-7, $6,000 flat. There's no card on file and nothing renews on its own. Cancel with one email, effective immediately. And your Profit Record keeps having to cover the bill: each invoice waits until what we've proven and found is ahead of everything invoiced, or it's void before it reaches you. When you leave, we true up: invoiced more than the Record shows, and the difference comes back to your bank.

What can you change in my account, exactly?

Only what your standing yes allows (the terms call it the standing mandate). That means bounded price steps, capped at 5% per cycle with your Buy Box (Amazon) or conversion rate (Shopify) watched while the step is live, plus ad corrections. Anything bigger needs your explicit yes first. And you hold a veto on everything.

What does the free month cost me, really?

If we deliver, we'll ask you for two things, once, on a private page we send you when the Profit Record has earned the asking: a short testimonial, and permission to publish your results with the numbers anonymized. Each is a separate yes, and no is fine. That's the whole price. On that page is also a link of your own — a founder you send gets the same free month, and when their first invoice stands after their own day 30, your next month is on us.

How do I revoke access?

Amazon: Settings → User Permissions → remove the user. Shopify: Settings → Users → Collaborators → remove. One click, effective instantly. And your data plus your full Profit Record export free, any time. Yes, including the day you cancel.

Who do I talk to?

The founder. Always. Reply to any Hubricon email and it lands with the person who builds your models and writes your briefs. There is no support tier.
