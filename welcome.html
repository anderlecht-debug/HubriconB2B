Answers you shouldn't have to ask for

When do I actually pay?

Never in month one. If you stay past day 30, the first invoice arrives by email — ACH, net-7, $6,000 flat. There is no card on file and nothing renews automatically against your will; cancel with one email, effective immediately.

What can you change in my account, exactly?

Only what the standing mandate we set on your kickoff call allows — bounded price steps (capped at 5% per cycle, Buy Box watched daily) and ad corrections. Anything outside those bounds needs your explicit yes first, and you hold a veto on everything.

What does the free month cost me, really?

If we deliver, we'll ask for a short testimonial and permission to publish your results with the numbers anonymized. That's the whole price of the seat.

How do I revoke access?

Settings → User Permissions → remove the user. One click, effective instantly. Your data and your full ledger export free, any time — including the day you cancel.

Who do I talk to?

The founder — always. Reply to any Hubricon email and it lands with the person who builds your models and records your briefs. There is no support tier.
