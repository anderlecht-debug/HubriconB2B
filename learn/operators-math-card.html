The Operator's Math · the course on one page

The operator's math, on one page

The rules and formulas of all five lessons, the same arithmetic Hubricon's engine runs. Margin rate is contribution before ads ÷ price.

The full courseThe spreadsheet (.xlsx)

1Contribution, not gross margin

contribution before ads = price − landed cost − payment fee − label − packing − refunds

Gross margin stops at the shelf. Contribution is what one order adds to the business; take off ad spend per order (a month's ad spend ÷ its orders) for what it adds after ads.

Lesson 1 →

2Your break-even

break-even ROAS = price ÷ contribution before ads

break-even ACOS = contribution before ads ÷ price

A target under the break-even buys every first order at a loss. ACOS is ROAS upside down.

Lesson 2 →

3The last dollar, not the average

A platform's ROAS is an average. The next dollar returns less than the average on a campaign that is already spending. Judge the last dollar against the break-even; past where it falls under, each dollar loses on a first order.

Lesson 2 →

4And the customers who come back

break-even with repeat customers = first-order break-even ÷ lifetime multiplier

Only on your own repeat orders, on margin, discounted, and checked against a quarter it did not see. On Amazon no export says who came back: use the first-order break-even.

Lesson 2 →

5LTV to CAC, counted honestly

CAC = ad spend in a month ÷ customers whose first order fell in it

LTV (52 weeks, margin) = margin rate × (first order + repeat orders × repeat value)

Margin, not revenue. A year, not a lifetime. A new customer, not the survivors. Say CAC is blended.

Lesson 3 →

6Payback

margin back by week t = margin rate × (first order + repeat orders by week t × repeat value)

Payback is the week it passes CAC. Until then, every new customer is cash out. A store can grow and still run out of cash when payback is longer than its cash lasts.

Lesson 4 →

7The numbers that mislead

  1. LTV to CAC on revenue: flatters.
  2. Break-even ROAS on the first order alone: too strict where customers come back.
  3. Gross margin: flatters.
  4. Average ROAS: flatters the last dollar.

Ranked on the course's invented store; on yours the order may differ.

Lesson 5 →

8In order

  1. Contribution before ads on your best seller.
  2. Break-even ROAS from it, and the last dollar on your largest campaign.
  3. Repeat orders and payback from your own Orders export.
