The Shopify Margin · the course on one page

Your Shopify margin, on one page

The rules and formulas of all six lessons. USPS Ground Advantage and Shopify Payments as published in 2026; check the live rates before you act on a figure.

The full courseThe spreadsheet (.xlsx)

1Where an order's money goes

kept before ads = price − Shopify Payments − the label − packing − landed cost

Shopify Payments: 2.9% of the whole charge plus $0.30 on Basic, less on larger plans; another gateway adds 2% on Basic. The label is USPS's card by weight and zone: plan on the far end until you know your zones.

Lesson 1 →

2The pound line

Under a pound is one flat rate. From a pound up, every part pound rounds up: 16.1 oz bills as 2 lb.

saved a month = (rate now − rate under the line) × orders

Weigh the packed parcel. Within about 3 oz of a line, trim the box, fill or insert; further is a redesign.

Lesson 2 →

3The free-shipping line

what the line costs a month = orders over the line × the average label

It pays only if the orders and baskets it brings keep that much more. Test it like a price: move it, measure four weeks against four.

Lesson 3 →

4The compare-at

A "was" price is a claim about the past. The FTC's guide asks that it was openly and actively offered, for a reasonably substantial period, in good faith (16 CFR 233.1).

Half or more of the catalogue under its own compare-at: the sale is your price. Offer it for real or take it off, then test the price itself.

Lesson 4 →

5The most an ad sale can cost

the most one ad sale can cost = kept before ads

break-even ROAS = price ÷ kept before ads

A campaign under it loses on every sale it buys. The platform's credit includes sales that would have happened anyway: keep a margin of safety.

Lesson 5 →

6A cheaper label lowers the bar

Every dollar off the label is a dollar more kept, so a parcel trimmed under a pound lowers the break-even ROAS on every campaign that sells it.

Repeat customers can carry a first order that loses a little: price that with your own repeat rate, never a hope.

Lesson 5 →

7Every variant, from one export

Shopify Admin, Products, Export: Variant Grams and Variant Compare At Price.

Grams may be the product alone: add the box and fill before you trust a line. The course reads the file in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

  2. The compare-at policy: real, or gone.
  3. The free-shipping line and the ad ceiling, on your own numbers.
