# Welcome video — ElevenLabs production script

~100 seconds · 7 segments. Generate each segment as its own audio clip
(retakes stay cheap, timing stays alignable), record the screen silently
following the shot notes, assemble in CapCut/Descript/iMovie, upload the
finished MP4 to Loom, paste the video id into `WELCOME_LOOM_ID` in
welcome.html.

## ElevenLabs settings

- Voice: a calm, unhurried professional male or neutral voice (avoid the
  energetic "YouTube" presets — this is a private bank, not a launch video).
- Stability ~60 · Similarity ~75 · Style low/off · Speed 0.95.
- The ellipses and commas below are deliberate — they are the pacing.

## Screen setup

Chrome, clean profile, bookmarks bar hidden, window ~1440px wide, browser
zoom 125%. The entire video is the live /welcome page — slow scrolls, a
deliberate cursor, nothing else. 1080p capture. Record each segment a
beat longer than the audio and trim.

---

### 1 · 0:00–0:12 — SCREEN: page top, headline + promise visible. No movement for 2s, then the slightest scroll drift.

> Welcome to Hubricon — and thank you for the trust. This page is your
> entire onboarding. One thing to do, one call to book... and everything
> else is ours to carry.

### 2 · 0:12–0:27 — SCREEN: slow scroll to rest on the promise paragraph; cursor sweeps under "execute the profit fixes for you".

> Here's the deal. We run the math on your Amazon account, and we
> execute the profit fixes for you. Pricing, ads, inventory... all of it.
> You watch a three-minute video every two weeks, and you keep the
> margin. Your first month? Free. If we don't find you more than we
> cost... you walk away owing nothing.

### 3 · 0:27–0:52 — SCREEN: scroll to Step 1. Hover the email box, click "Copy email" (button flips to "Copied ✓" on camera), then move the cursor down the four permission rows, pausing on each; end hovering the red "everything else stays off" row.

> Step one is the only thing we will ever need from you. In Seller
> Central, go to Settings, then User Permissions, and invite this email
> address. When the invite is accepted, grant exactly the four
> permissions listed here — business reports, fulfillment reports,
> pricing, and campaign manager. Nothing else. Banking and account
> settings stay untouchable, and you can revoke the seat any day, in one
> click. That seat is how our models read your numbers, and how
> corrections get executed — you will never export a report for us.

### 4 · 0:52–1:02 — SCREEN: cursor rests on the "one five-minute homework" paragraph.

> One small homework, once. Amazon can't tell us what your product costs
> you — so there's a five-minute template in your welcome email. One row
> per SKU. Estimates are fine.

### 5 · 1:02–1:20 — SCREEN: scroll to Step 2; let the Calendly calendar render fully; cursor circles a date without clicking.

> Step two — book your kickoff. Within twenty-four hours of your seat
> going live, your Profit Teardown gets recorded: every fee decomposed,
> your demand curves fitted, and the leaks priced in dollars. On the
> kickoff call we present your ninety-day plan, and we set your standing
> mandate — what we execute without asking, and where your veto sits.

### 6 · 1:20–1:34 — SCREEN: scroll to the Step 3 timeline; cursor steps down the rows in rhythm with the narration, landing on "Every 2 weeks".

> From there, the rhythm is simple. Every two weeks, a three-minute video
> lands in your inbox — what we found, what we fixed, and what it
> earned, with the running ledger total. That is your entire time cost.

### 7 · 1:34–1:52 — SCREEN: pause on the "Day 30" row, then slow scroll through the FAQ to the footer; hold the footer for the last 3 seconds.

> And the terms stay exactly as promised. No card on file. Nothing renews
> against your will. Your first invoice arrives at day thirty — only if
> you stay. Questions, any time: reply to any email, and it lands with
> the founder. Welcome aboard... let's go find the margin.
