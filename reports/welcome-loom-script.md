# Welcome Loom — 90-second narration script

Record screen-on-the-welcome-page (no face needed — cursor over each step as you
speak). Target 80–95 seconds. Paste the share URL's id into `WELCOME_LOOM_ID`
at the bottom of welcome.html when done.

---

**[On the headline]**
Welcome aboard — and thank you for the trust. This one page is your entire
onboarding: one thing to do, one call to book, and everything else is ours.

**[Scroll to Step 1]**
The only thing we ever need from you is this: add this email to your Seller
Central under Settings, User Permissions. Grant exactly the four permissions
listed — reports, pricing, advertising. Nothing else. Banking and account
settings stay untouchable, and you can revoke the seat with one click any day.
That seat is how our models read your numbers and how we execute corrections
for you — you will never export a spreadsheet for us.

**[Scroll to Step 2]**
Within twenty-four hours of that seat going live, you'll have your Profit
Teardown on video — every fee decomposed, your demand curves fitted, and the
dollars your account is leaking, named. Book your kickoff here: on that call I
present your ninety-day plan and we agree on the standing mandate — what we
execute without asking, and where your veto sits.

**[Scroll to Step 3 / timeline]**
From there it's simple: every two weeks a three-minute video lands in your
inbox — what we found, what we executed, what it earned, with the running
ledger total. That's your whole time cost.

**[Scroll to the day-30 line]**
And the terms are exactly as promised: your first month is free. If we haven't
found you more than we cost, walk away owing nothing — no card on file, nothing
renews against your will. If we have — and we intend to — the first invoice
comes day thirty, and we keep compounding from there.

Talk soon.
