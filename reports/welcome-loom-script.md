# Welcome video — ElevenLabs script, cut for 1:30

~214 words, 7 segments, targets a 90-second read. No ellipses anywhere:
ElevenLabs renders "..." as one to two seconds of dead air, which is how
the first take ballooned to 2:27. Pauses come from periods and commas only.

## ElevenLabs settings (changed from v1)

- Speed: 1.0 (not 0.95).
- Stability ~50 · Similarity ~75 · Style low/off.
- Generate each segment as its own clip; when stitching, butt the clips
  tight — trim any leading/trailing silence ElevenLabs adds (half a
  second per clip across seven clips is another five seconds gone).
