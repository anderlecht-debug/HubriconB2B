How this works

One simulated supplier, one year. Each thin line is an order, blue once it passes its promised date. Below it: orders waiting at the supplier, all brands. The supplier promises 20 days; production takes 12; the rest is waiting in line. The forecast uses that line plus the Lunar New Year calendar, and the real date lands inside it about 8 times in 10. Why the delay can be forecast

Late suppliers are busy suppliers.

  1. Your supplier works for many brands.When their orders pile up, yours waits. At 90% busy, the wait is about 9× longer than at 50%.
  2. No brand can see the whole line alone.Brands that share a supplier can. Your order dates are enough.
  3. Late stays late.A supplier over 80% busy was still late 3 months on (simulation). That makes it forecastable.

How these are worked out

Sales lost: how late the worst 1 in 10 orders runs at a supplier this busy (typical of 200 simulated), times a day of that supplier's sales. Extra stock covers the forecast miss 9 times in 10: 10.4 days of product cost knowing only the season, 7.2 days with 5 brands pooled (Fig. 3).
