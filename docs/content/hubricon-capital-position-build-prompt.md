# Capital Position — second tier build prompt

Companion to `hubricon-landing-rework-prompt.md`. That document reworks the Managed Profit
page. This one adds the tier below it and amends three things in that document.

---

## 0 · Sequencing gate — read before building

**Do not build this until three Managed Profit clients are paying.**

Two offers, two funnels and two support loads on one operator with zero revenue is the most
common way a business like this dies. Capital Position is also meant to be built from what real
accounts taught you, not from assumptions about what $1M sellers want — including the core
assumption this whole tier rests on, that the purchase-order wire is the pain. That is
currently a guess.

What ships **now**, in a day, is only the capture described in §3. Everything else waits.

---

## 1 · The name and what it has to do

**Capital Position.** The weekly artifact is **the Monday Position**. The opening artifact stays
the **Profit Teardown**, shared with Managed Profit, so the two tiers read as rungs of one thing
rather than two products.

Naming rules that govern every line of copy:

- It sits *below* Managed Profit and must never sound better than it. Position is what you
  **know**. Managed Profit is what gets **done**. That hierarchy is the whole ladder and no copy
  should be needed to explain it.
- It reframes inventory as capital allocation. A founder checking his capital position before a
  wire has been promoted; a founder placing a reorder has not. The identity shift is what makes
  $6,000 defensible.
- Register matches the house system — Profit Teardown, Profit Record, Managed Profit, Capital
  Position. Plain institutional nouns, no adjectives.

**Banned vocabulary across the entire tier:** Desk, Lite, Essentials, Starter, Basic, Pro, Plus,
Tier 2. Every one of them tells the customer he bought the lesser thing. Also avoid "reorder" as
a product-level word — it drags the price anchor toward $49/mo software. It survives only inside
feature descriptions.

Tagline: **Know your position before you wire.**

Run a trademark and domain check before committing.

## 2 · The offer

**Capital Position — $6,000 a year, paid up front. No monthly option.**

Same number as Managed Profit, different unit. One price list, two rungs, nothing to memorize.

Positioning anchor, and this governs every line of copy: **the fee is priced against the wire,
not against the client's revenue.** A $1.5M brand makes four to eight purchase orders a year at
$40k–$100k each, wired on a spreadsheet guess. $6,000 to price a year of those decisions is a
rounding error beside the decisions. $6,000 as "inventory software" sits next to Helium 10 and
loses. Never let the copy put it next to a SaaS price.

### Included

1. **The opening Profit Teardown** — true net margin per SKU, every fee decomposed, landed cost,
   ads honestly allocated. Same artifact as the free Teardown, delivered in 24 hours.
2. **Every purchase order, sized and dated** — 20,000 simulations per SKU, stockout probability,
   the date, the quantity, **the wire amount**, and the service level each SKU's own margin
   justifies rather than a flat 95%.
3. **Your cash trough** — 90 days forward, the worst day, how deep it goes, and the odds of
   hitting zero. `P(ruin)` and `ES₅[trough]`.
4. **Your claims, dated** — valued at landed cost, with the day each window shuts. *They* file.
   They keep 100%. A recovery service keeps roughly a quarter.
5. **The Monday Position** — one page a week: what changed, what the anomaly scan caught, what
   needs a decision this week.

### Explicitly excluded, stated in these words on the page

No moves made in their account. No price steps, no ad cuts, no claims filed, no orders placed.
No Profit Record. No proven-or-void. No founder on a call. No fortnightly video.

The gap is the product. Do not close it, do not soften it, do not apologize for it. The month the
gap stops being acceptable is the month they buy Managed Profit.

### Guarantee

One layer only: **if the opening Teardown finds nothing worth fixing, we say so and refund in
full.** After the Teardown is delivered the year is non-refundable. It does not auto-renew — no
card is stored, and renewal at month twelve is a fresh invoice they choose to pay. The four-layer
proven-or-void stack does not travel down and must not appear anywhere on this page.

## 6 · Engine and operations

Everything this tier delivers already exists. It is a restricted client type, not new models.

Implement as a `plan` flag on the client record — **`position` | `managed`** — that enforces:

- **No directives, ever.** A `position` client never has a directive issued, never opens a veto
  window, never has anything executed. Guard this in `issue.py` at the source, not by convention.
- **No Profit Record.** Ledger and value computation disabled; the portal must not render the
  section for this plan.
- **Read-only access.** Amazon seat is Reports-view only — no Pricing, no Campaign Manager.
  Shopify collaborator view-only, no Products or Discounts edit. This lowers the objection *and*
  enforces the exclusion structurally. Exports-only onboarding stays available.
- **Weekly cadence**, page only — no video, no narrated letter, no founder memo.
  `ISSUE_INTERVAL_DAYS` becomes plan-dependent.
- **Modules enabled:** `MARGIN.DECOMP`, `MONTE_CARLO.RUN`, `NEWSVENDOR`, cash runway,
  `ANOMALY.SCAN`, `RECOVERY.EV` (listed, never filed). **Computed but not surfaced:**
  `ELASTICITY.FIT`, `PRICE.OPTIMUM`, `SPEND.RESPONSE` — these feed only the ascension line in §7.

**Billing:** annual, invoiced once, ACH, no card stored, no auto-renew.

**The hard operating rule:** no deliverable in this tier may require the founder to look at a
specific account. If it cannot ship while he is asleep, it does not belong in Capital Position.
Any feature request that fails this test is declined, or becomes a reason to quote Managed Profit.

Add `/position-terms` mirroring `/terms`: what is delivered, what is excluded, the Teardown
refund, non-refundable after delivery, no auto-renew, export on request, the read-only scope.

Rename in code and copy: any `desk`/`reorder_desk` identifier becomes `position`. Check
`portal.html`'s existing "position hero" block for naming collisions — it can stay, but the
plan-level noun and the UI element should not read as the same object.

## 7 · Ascension

This is why the tier exists and it must be built, not left to sales instinct.

Every Monday Position ends with one line naming money the client is not permitted to touch, drawn
from the computed-but-not-surfaced modules:

> This week we found $4,200/mo of ad spend with zero attributed sales and two SKUs that can carry
> a price increase. Execution is Managed Profit.

Rules: state the dollars, never the detail. No search terms listed, no SKUs named, no target
prices. It is an honest description of a real boundary, not a tease — the number is true, and the
reason they can't act on it is that they bought the tier that doesn't act.

Two automatic Managed Profit invitations, sent by the system rather than from memory:
- Trailing-twelve-month revenue crosses $3M
- Cumulative ascension-line dollars exceed $6,000/mo for three consecutive months
