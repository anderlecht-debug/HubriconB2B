Method, assumptions, and what public data can't see
  • Read from the listing's public page on September 3, 2026: price band, item weight, dimensions (large standard size) and sales rank (top 100 in Automotive). Priced on September 30, 2026.
  • Fees: Amazon's published 2026 US fulfilment schedules, January 15 to October 14 and October 15 to January 14, plus the 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge; a 12% referral fee.
  • The item weight is a floor. Amazon bills the packed weight, so the unit sits at least this far past the edge.
  • Volume: about 3,500 units a month from the public sales rank, a curve that can be wrong by half or double; simulated that wide. This one listing: an estimated $0.5M to $1.6M a year in sales.
  • Landed cost: assumed at 25% of price, drifting 10% either way each month. No public page shows it.
  • Simulation: 10,000 years of twelve months from October 2026, each month on the card in force. Year-end profit: P10 $230,000, median $402,000, P90 $687,000.
  • Storage and aged-inventory rates: Amazon's schedule effective January 15, 2026.
  • What public data can't see: advertising, returns, the real landed cost, the packed weight, and how long each unit has sat.
